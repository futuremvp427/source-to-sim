# NHL backup — frozen DATA REHEARSAL v1

Authorized October 14, 2026; study kalshi-nhl-data-rehearsal-20261014-v1.

This starts the previously prepared backup's first stage NOW: durable forward capture and infrastructure qualification. It does not score strategy profit, simulate fills, open positions or change the BTC study. P&L and fill fields are null, not zero-profit strategy results. Requested 10/25/50 sizes and maker/taker comparisons remain the subsequent design, not six independent wins.

Collection lasts 48 hours from the workflow bootstrap's actual UTC start, which is written in state and launch artifacts. Nine sequential jobs hand state through exact preceding artifacts, with checkpoints every approximately nine minutes plus publication overhead. No heartbeat is inferred from running-job status. No automatic rerun or fallback to an earlier checkpoint.

Only public GET endpoints from external-api.kalshi.com and NHL score/game-center LANDING endpoints are allowed. The play-by-play endpoint returned 403 in local preflight and is deliberately excluded; no access-control workaround. No venue credentials, orders, private endpoints, paid AI calls, incentives, subscriptions or deposits. Standard Ubuntu runners in this public repository are used; no paid larger runner or dependency on production services. Artifact retention five days; no Actions cache. Raw capture has a 240 MB uncompressed cap; hitting it ends collection and marks insufficient coverage, not success.

Game eligibility: NHL gameType=2, canonical one-contract representation of a two-team game, matched by league ID, date and team abbreviations. Capture future schedules and official metadata; track live third-period book/tape paths from <=15 minutes remaining. Evaluate readiness in the prospective 0<clock<=10-minute region. Ten game-clock minutes is not wall-clock TTE to close. Scheduled or expected market expiration never substitutes for the game clock.

Freeze: target cadence five seconds in period 3, otherwise 30 seconds live and 60 seconds pregame; discovery every five minutes; network pacing four requests/second; book round-trip <=3 seconds; verified source/receipt/tape freshness <=10 seconds; complete fixed-point two-sided book, spread <=3 cents; full paginated interval tape, trade-ID deduplication, off-book prints excluded; series maker fees captured, not assumed zero. Public clock downloads are parallel, while journal/state changes are serial for hash safety. Slow responses or concurrent games may miss cadence; missed cycles and gaps count against coverage. Cadence is not guaranteed.

Clock freshness requires an actual documented source update/event timestamp, not HTTP Date, receipt time or unchanged clock response. Current unverified feed semantics are an explicit launch limitation: capture continues, decisions remain invalid. protocol.clock_source_semantics_verified is frozen false for this rehearsal, so it cannot certify trading readiness by accident even if a timestamp-shaped field appears. This rehearsal may honestly end INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE. No rules are loosened to produce fills. Source-time field candidates are logged for later adapter validation, not proof that the timestamp describes clock freshness. A separately identified follow-up can qualify a documented adapter using new forward observations; this capture remains data-only.

Readiness minimum: >=5 complete independent games; third-period 10-minute boundary captured at >=590 seconds and final boundary at <=10 seconds; no candidate gap over ten seconds; >=95% valid planned candidate cycles per game, including cadence misses; official finalized/settled binary result recorded. Rehearsal requires collection to end before readiness verdict. Unsupported cancellation/fair-price results are not binary wins. Games with unknown clock timing, missing mapping, missing settlements or freshness failure do not count as complete. Final status is READY_FOR_SEPARATE_PREREG_REVIEW or INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE. The latter is a data verdict, not strategy rejection.

Data incidents: no valid decisions for five minutes during a live third period is recorded; checkpoint age >20 minutes is a publication incident. The independent read-only health checker verifies manifest and raw chain, reports actual timestamps and preserves unresolved results. External review is separate; it must not claim monitoring alerts are installed until its schedule is confirmed.

Hash all raw request/response and observation records, chain across exact resumptions, hash state/summary/status/raw files in every manifest. Pinned protocol/code hash and workflow run/attempt must match. The raw tape is replay/audit material, not a permission to backfill missed decision-time entries. No strategy outcomes from this data-only rehearsal enter a later forward P&L study.

After readiness, a separately preregistered paper study may use the original proposed late-game favorite mechanism with maker/taker and 10/25/50 accounts. It is not started automatically, and rules, sample targets, fees, queue assumptions and chronology must be fully frozen first. Original BTC rules, production/default branch and all prior studies remain untouched.
